# 📚 Study Command Center - Student Guide

> Your personal study companion that keeps you organized, accountable, and on track to ace your exams.

---

## What Is the Study Command Center?

The Study Command Center is a web app built specifically for college students who juggle multiple subjects, attend tuition classes, and need to stay on top of self-study and revisions. Think of it as your digital study planner that:

- Tracks what you studied in college and tuition today
- Tells you exactly what to study tonight
- Automatically schedules revisions so you never forget what you learned
- Flags topics you are falling behind on
- Gives you a clear weekend catch-up plan

No more sticky notes, forgotten topics, or last-minute panic before exams. Let's walk through how to use it every single day.

---

## 🌅 Your Daily Workflow

### Morning Routine (5 minutes)

1. **Open the Dashboard** (`/`)
   - Glance at your daily completion score from yesterday
   - Check how many revision topics are due today
   - Note any overdue or urgent pending topics flagged in red
   - Look at today's focus section to see what is already planned

2. **Check Today's Study** (`/today`)
   - This page auto-generates your study plan for the day based on:
     - Revision topics due (spaced repetition)
     - Overdue high-priority pending topics
     - Tasks you manually added
   - Review the list and mentally prepare for the day ahead

> **Pro tip:** Use the Kanban board view (toggle in top-right) if you like dragging tasks between columns like "Not Started", "Studying Now", and "Completed".

---

### After College / Classes (10 minutes)

3. **Log What Was Taught** (`/log`)
   - Open the Daily Log page
   - For each topic covered in college today, fill in:
     - Subject and chapter
     - Topic title
     - Source: "College"
     - Did you understand it? (Yes / Somewhat / No)
     - Status: Completed, Need to Study Tonight, or Pending
   - Toggle "Advanced Mode" if you want to record page numbers, teacher name, difficulty, or confusion details
   - Hit save after each entry

4. **Log Tuition Topics** (same page)
   - Repeat the same process but set the source to "Tuition"
   - This keeps your college and tuition learning streams separate

> **Why this matters:** Logging takes 2 minutes per topic but saves you hours of confusion later. The app uses these logs to auto-schedule revisions and flag what needs attention tonight.

---

### Evening Self-Study Session (use throughout)

5. **Come Back to Today's Study** (`/today`)
   - Your evening plan is now populated with:
     - Topics marked "Need to Study Tonight" from your logs
     - Revision topics that are due
     - Any overdue topics you need to catch up on
   - Click "Start" on a task when you begin studying it
   - When done, click "Mark Complete" and rate your mastery (1-5 stars)
   - If you cannot finish something, click "Pending" to defer it

6. **Night Mode** (toggle on Today's Study page)
   - Switch on Night Mode for a minimal dark interface
   - Shows only your remaining tasks in a distraction-free list
   - Tap the circle next to a task to mark it complete
   - Perfect for late-night study sessions when you want focus

---

### Before Bed (2 minutes)

7. **Quick check on the Dashboard**
   - See your daily completion percentage
   - Feel good about what you accomplished
   - Note if anything critical spilled over to tomorrow

## 📖 Feature Guide - When to Use Each Page

### Dashboard (`/`)
**When:** Start and end of each day
**What it does:** Shows your bird's-eye view of everything - total topics, completion score, pending count, revision due, and subject-wise progress with mastery stars.

---

### Today's Study (`/today`)
**When:** Morning planning + evening study sessions
**What it does:** Your daily action list. Auto-generates tasks from revisions due, overdue topics, and logged items. Supports list view and Kanban board view. Has Night Mode for focused sessions.

---

### Daily Log (`/log`)
**When:** After every class (college or tuition)
**What it does:** Quick-entry form to log what was taught. Records subject, chapter, topic, understanding level, and status. This is the primary data entry point that powers everything else.

---

### Subjects & Chapters (`/subjects`)
**When:** Start of semester or when a new chapter begins
**What it does:** Manage your subjects and their chapters. Add subjects with color codes. Add chapters within each subject. Track completion percentage and mastery per chapter.

**Setup tip:** At the start of the semester, add all your subjects and their chapter names. This makes daily logging much faster since you just pick from dropdowns.

---

### College Tracker (`/college`)
**When:** Weekly review or when checking college-specific progress
**What it does:** Filtered view of all topics logged from college lectures. Filter by date range, subject, understanding level, or status. See which college topics need attention.

---

### Tuition Tracker (`/tuition`)
**When:** Reviewing tuition progress or checking overlap with college
**What it does:** Same as College Tracker but filtered for tuition-source logs. Helps you see if tuition is covering what college missed or vice versa.

---

### Self-Study Tracker (`/self-study`)
**When:** Planning and tracking independent study sessions
**What it does:** Create self-study plans with goals, target dates, and specific topics. Track resources you need. Monitor progress on topics you are studying on your own initiative.

---

### Pending Topics (`/pending`)
**When:** When you feel overwhelmed or need to prioritize
**What it does:** Shows all topics that are incomplete, not understood, or deferred. Grouped by urgency (>14 days old = urgent, >7 days = old). Lets you resolve, delete, or re-prioritize pending items. Available in Kanban view too.

**Key insight:** This page is your "debt tracker." Keep it as short as possible. If something has been pending for 2+ weeks, either tackle it this weekend or decide it is not worth your time.

---

### Revision Queue (`/revision`)
**When:** Daily (to see what is due) + after completing a revision
**What it does:** Spaced repetition scheduler. Shows topics due today, overdue, due this week, and categorizes your topics as "strong" or "weak." When you revise a topic, rate your recall and it automatically schedules the next revision.

**How spacing works:**
- Rate mastery 1-2 stars: Revise again in 1-2 days
- Rate mastery 3 stars: Revise again in 3-5 days
- Rate mastery 4 stars: Revise again in 1-2 weeks
- Rate mastery 5 stars: Revise again in 3-4 weeks

---

### Weekly Catch-Up (`/weekly`)
**When:** Friday evening or Saturday morning
**What it does:** Automatically collects all the topics that fell behind during the week - pending items, things you did not understand, deferred tasks. Generates a weekend study plan split across Friday and Saturday. Mark items complete as you work through them.

---

### Calendar (`/calendar`)
**When:** Planning ahead or looking back at study history
**What it does:** Visual calendar showing your study activity. See which days were productive, spot gaps, and plan future study sessions.

---

### Analytics (`/analytics`)
**When:** Weekly or whenever you want motivation
**What it does:** Charts and insights about your study habits. Tracks daily activity trends, subject-wise progress, mastery distribution, study health score, and streak data.

**Metrics to watch:**
- Study Health Score (aim for 75+)
- Subject radar chart (balanced = good)
- Daily completion trend line (keep it consistent)

---

### Settings (`/settings`)
**When:** Initial setup + occasional tweaks
**What it does:** Manage subjects and their colors, configure revision intervals, set study days, import/export your data, and toggle dark mode.

## 💡 Best Practices for Staying Consistent

### 1. Log immediately after class
The #1 habit that makes this system work. If you wait until evening, you will forget details. It takes 30 seconds per topic.

### 2. Be honest about understanding
Marking "Yes" when you only "Somewhat" understood wastes your future self's time. The app uses this to decide what needs revision.

### 3. Trust the revision schedule
When the app says revise something, do it. Spaced repetition is scientifically proven to boost long-term retention. Skipping revisions creates a snowball of overdue items.

### 4. Use Night Mode for focus
When studying in the evening, switch to Night Mode on Today's Study. The minimal interface removes distractions and lets you check off tasks one by one.

### 5. Keep the Pending list short
Treat pending topics like email inbox zero. Review it every Sunday. If something has been there for 3+ weeks, either do it now or drop it.

### 6. Check Analytics weekly, not daily
Analytics are for spotting trends over time. Checking daily can lead to obsessing over numbers instead of actually studying.

### 7. Set up subjects early
Spend 15 minutes at the start of the semester adding all subjects and chapters. This investment pays off every single day when logging is just a few taps.

### 8. Use the Quick Add shortcut
The Quick Add dialog lets you rapidly log items without navigating to the full Daily Log form. Use it when you just need to quickly note something.

### 9. Rate mastery honestly after revision
When the completion dialog asks for your mastery rating, be truthful. Rating yourself 5 stars when you struggled means the next revision gets scheduled too far out and you will forget.

### 10. Do not skip weekends
The Weekly Catch-Up feature exists because we all fall behind during busy weekdays. Use weekends to clear your backlog. Even 1-2 hours on Saturday can keep you from drowning.
